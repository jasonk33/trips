Good to know

Know before you go

This run did not research lodging supply; the zone agents cover it. Every stay carries the same stacked Palm Beach County tax (6% state, a county surtax, 6% county tourist tax), about 12.5% on the pre-tax price, and rentals usually need it added on top. Oceanfront Park is basic: paid parking, a concession for chairs and umbrellas, and lifeguards only from 9 am to 5 pm. Peak-season demand and February weather were not sourced in this run.

February weather and water
  • February highs average about 75 to 77 F, and lows rarely fall below 67 F (via search snippet from Weatherblaze; not fetched).
  • Sea water is about 76 to 77 F in February, comfortable for swimming (via search snippet from seatemperature.info; not fetched).
  • Sargassum and seaweed timing for February was not researched; check before relying on the water.

Sources: weatherblaze.com seatemperature.info

Beach hours and lifeguards
  • Oceanfront Park's beach is open 365 days a year from sunrise to 9 pm.
  • Lifeguards are on duty daily from 9 am to 5 pm; the city lists no lifeguard coverage outside those hours.

Sources: boynton-beach.org

Chairs and umbrellas
  • Chairs and umbrellas are rented, not provided; the city points to the concession Oceanside Beach Service.
  • Set-ups are two chairs with cushions, a table and an umbrella, or a cabana. Prices run from $15 per hour to $700 for an annual pass, with multi-day rates. The city's contact number is 561-840-3373.
  • How easy chairs are to get in peak season was not researched.

Sources: boynton-beach.org

Parking at Oceanfront Park
  • Parking is paid. Per the Island Watersports guide: $3.50 an hour, or a flat $5 per vehicle from May 1 to Nov 15 and $10 from Nov 16 to Apr 30. The trip dates fall in the $10 flat window.
  • The same guide lists an annual permit at $65.
  • The rates come from a retailer's guide, not the city; confirm on the city site before you arrive.

Sources: islandwatersports.com

Dogs on the beach
  • Dogs are not allowed on the beach, except at the Oceanfront Bark meetup: the third Saturday of each month from November to February, 9 am to noon (per the Island Watersports guide, which is undated).
  • The third Saturday in February 2027 is 20 February, inside the trip, so expect dogs on the sand that morning.

Sources: islandwatersports.com

Rental registration and tax accounts
  • Palm Beach County requires a Short-Term Rental Local Business Tax Receipt for each tourist tax account. The owner or manager files a monthly tourist tax return, due on the 1st and late after the 20th.
  • Per Surge, citing DBPR: a whole-home rental rented more than three times a year for stays under 30 days needs a Florida DBPR vacation rental license, with a $50 application fee, a $10 education fee and $170 a year for a single unit.
  • Ask the host for the DBPR license number and the county receipt. A listing that shows neither is a red flag.
  • Every price on this page already includes each place’s own taxes and fees, so totals compare like for like.

Sources: pbctax.gov gowithsurge.com
