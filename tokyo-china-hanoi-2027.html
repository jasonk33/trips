How to think about it

  • Direction beats distance. Westbound flights (US → Asia, Asia → Europe, Europe → US) leave in the day and land the same afternoon. Asia → US flights are overnight or cost you a night — so don't come home across the Pacific.
  • Europe = Heathrow. The only daytime flights from Hanoi or Hong Kong to Europe land at Heathrow, so ending in London avoids an extra flight. Frankfurt → NYC (DL107) is $655 vs $678 from London, but getting to Frankfurt means another flight and another hotel move.
  • More China via the 240-hour transit rule. Fly into Shanghai from Japan and leave mainland China to Vietnam or Hong Kong within 10 days — no visa needed for you. Shanghai → Beijing is a 4.5-hour bullet train, not a flight.
  • Some July flights aren't on sale yet. Vietnam Airlines has loaded its China → Hanoi flights only through April–May 2027, so VN513 and VN533 show that date's fare (same summer times). Each has a Hong Kong fallback that is on sale now.
  • No departures before 10:00am. Going west, you adjust by staying up later. One exception is flagged: VN55 on Wednesdays leaves 9:55am.
  • Book separate one-way tickets. Far cheaper than one multi-city ticket. Every flight here has a night before the next one, so a delay can't cascade.
  • Pick airlines by seat. Standard economy pitch: JAL 33", Cathay / Vietnam Airlines 32", US airlines 31", China Southern A321 29" (short flight).
