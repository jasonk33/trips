How to think about it

  • Go to Hawaii from the West Coast. Saturday New York → Honolulu nonstops are $1,149+. Saturday to SF ($364) plus the evening nonstop SF → Honolulu ($424) is $687, and the SF nights are free.
  • Saturday costs almost nothing extra. Southwest's 12:50pm Saturday flight via Kansas City is $364 vs $349 for Friday's 6:30am nonstop. Saturday nonstops are $399 (lands 12:58am), $509 (8:10pm) or $529 (5:25pm).
  • The 8:06pm SF → Honolulu nonstop is the sweet spot. It's $424 on both Dec 22 and Dec 23, leaves after a full day with friends, and every other SF-area nonstop to Honolulu those days is $461 or more.
  • Meet Jason's group through Hilo, not Kona. On Dec 26 they move to the volcano side. Honolulu → Hilo that evening is $135; Honolulu → Kona is $185+. Hilo is 30 miles (45 min) from the park.
  • Fly out of Kona on Dec 29, with them. Kona has the nonstops to LA (3:20pm and 4:30pm); Hilo has none that day.
  • No early flights. Every flight in both plans leaves at 12:50pm or later; the only morning options are optional swaps.
  • Book each leg as its own one-way. Five separate tickets on Southwest, Alaska, American and JetBlue, with at least a night between flights so one delay can't wreck the next leg.
  • Lowest fare = basic economy. The 'Lowest fare' column includes basic fares; 'Standard economy' excludes them ($193 more). All five lowest fares still include a carry-on.

Before you book

1
4 SF + 3 Waikiki, or 3 SF + 4 Waikiki?
Same $1,630 in flights. Plan A has one more free night in SF; Plan B has one more paid night and surf day in Waikiki.
2
Big Island = the volcano side
Jason's group is in Kona Dec 22–26 and on the volcano side Dec 26–29, so 3 nights ending with the Dec 29 flight means the volcano side (sleeping in Hilo or Volcano village).
3
Ride with Jason's group or rent a car?
The park has no public transport or reliable rideshare, and Dec 29 means driving 95 miles to Kona airport. Riding with the group is simplest.
4
Late arrivals
Honolulu at 11:59pm: the W Line bus stops at 10:30pm, so take Route 42 or a rideshare, and confirm the Waikiki hostel's late check-in. Hilo at 9:50pm is fine — Howzit Hostels has 24-hour reception.
